About

A scholar and mom who makes
the Bible come alive.

Shelby Hanson holds a Master’s in Biblical Studies. She’s a homeschool mom raising three kids at home with her husband Nate. Faith, learning, and family life meet every day at the same kitchen table. She brings depth and clarity to biblical scholarship, making it accessible and alive for churches, conferences, classrooms, and other families teaching at home.

The riches of biblical scholarship belong to every Christ-follower, not just seminaries and Bible colleges.

Homeschool

Helping other moms teach
with confidence at home.

Homeschooling is part of who Shelby is, not a side note. Alongside her speaking and teaching, she walks with other parents who want to raise their kids at home but feel the weight of planning, lessons, and records. That’s why she and Nate built Arbor: a whole-year homeschool tool that carries the hard parts so moms can stay at the table with their kids. Nate built it; Shelby lives it daily and helps families find their footing.
