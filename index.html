Clackamas County Right to Life

Building a county where every life is welcomed.

Shelby is the director of Clackamas County Right to Life, the Clackamas County chapter of Oregon Right to Life. The chapter stands for every innocent human life, from conception to natural death.

Shelby is building out the chapter now, and she is looking for neighbors, churches, and families who want to help. If that is you, reach out to her directly below.

About Shelby

A scholar and mom who teaches the Bible with clarity.

Shelby holds a Master’s in Biblical Studies and loves reading Scripture in its original languages, especially Hebrew. She is a homeschool mom raising three kids with her husband Nate, and she makes biblical scholarship clear enough for churches, conferences, classrooms, and families who learn at home.

The riches of biblical scholarship belong to every Christ-follower, not just seminaries and Bible colleges.
