About

A scholar who makes
the Bible come alive.

Shelby Hanson holds a Master’s in Biblical Studies and co-hosts the Faith Lab podcast. She brings depth and clarity to biblical scholarship — making it accessible and alive for churches, conferences, and classrooms.

The riches of biblical scholarship belong to every Christ-follower — not just seminaries and Bible colleges.
