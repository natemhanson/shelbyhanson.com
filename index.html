About

A scholar and mom who teaches
the Bible with clarity.

Shelby Hanson holds a Master’s in Biblical Studies. She’s a homeschool mom raising three kids at home with her husband Nate. She teaches from that same kitchen table, and she makes biblical scholarship clear enough for churches, conferences, classrooms, and other families who learn at home.

The riches of biblical scholarship belong to every Christ-follower, not just seminaries and Bible colleges.

Homeschool

Homeschool help from a mom
who does it every day.

Shelby homeschools her three kids. She knows how heavy the planning, lessons, and records can get, and she helps other parents who want to teach at home without drowning in the logistics. That’s why she and Nate built Arbor: a whole-year homeschool tool that handles the admin so moms can stay with their kids. Nate built it. Shelby uses it every day, and she helps families get started.
